Content

Pre-class readings, lecture notes, and hands-on material for Climate Change Governance: Science, Data & Models (ENGG562/ENV462), organized by module. The course runs as a flipped classroom: complete the pre-class reading before each session, since class time is used for discussion, tool sessions, and case studies rather than lecture delivery.

This section is separate from the course logistics page — see Course Info for the full week and module schedule and assessment and policy detail, and Readings & Tools for the full reading list.

Modules¶

  • Module 1: Climate Science Foundations — Weeks 1–3

  • Module 2: Scenarios & Integrated Assessment — Weeks 4–6

  • Module 3: Policy Landscape — Weeks 7–8

  • Module 4: Energy–Water–Agriculture Nexus — Weeks 9–13

  • Capstone — Project Presentations and Defense — Week 14

Each module page holds that module’s lecture slides and any interactive tools used in class.
