# Module 1: Climate Science Foundations

**Weeks 1–3**

This module establishes the physical basis of the greenhouse effect and the history of its discovery and confirmation. By the end of Week 3, students should be able to interpret an observational temperature record and identify the forcing responsible for the observed trend.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain Earth's energy balance and the carbon cycle.
- Trace the development of climate science from Fourier's 1824 hypothesis through Hansen's 1988 congressional testimony to CMIP7.
- Interpret observational climate data and distinguish forcing, feedback, and noise.

## Guiding Question

What evidence distinguishes anthropogenic warming from natural climate variability?
